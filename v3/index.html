About CJLO

Hey! We're CJLO 1690AM, Concordia University's radio station, broadcasting from the Loyola campus in Montréal's west end. Volunteer DJs and hosts run almost everything you hear.

Turn us on for weird and wonderful music, local news and talk, and live sessions from bands passing through town.

Who CJLO'd like to meet

Local bands with a demo, students who want to learn radio, night owls, record collectors, and anyone with a show idea. Come say hi on the 4th floor.
